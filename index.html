The Tale of the
Grizzled Warrior and His Princess

A Chronicle, Lovingly Exaggerated
↓ unroll the scroll ↓

Chapter I · The Meeting

Once upon a time, in the distant year of 2016, three brave souls met a grizzled warrior, as old as time itself, in the legendary halls of PG.

Through years of battles, Account Managers, and questionable decisions, the ancient warrior became not only a trusted colleague, but a dear friend.

Chapter II · The Kazan

His weapon of choice was not a sword, nor an axe, but a humongous cup of tea, known throughout the realm as The Kazan. Many had tried to drink from it. Few survived.

Many seasons passed.

Chapter III · The Journey

The warrior travelled far beyond the borders of Bulgaria, into the mysterious lands of Austria. And there, having already known his princess from times long before the three companions entered his story, he returned to her side.

Together, they built a kingdom of their own.

Meanwhile, the three loyal companions remained behind, bravely defending the homeland and patiently awaiting the warrior's annual return.

Chapter IV · The Wedding

And then, after many adventures, the great day of their wedding finally arrived.

The three companions received the news with great joy.

They also received the distinct honour of not being invited.

A decision so bold that even the Red Wedding might have considered it excessive. 🥲

Still, like true friends — and much like the Ferrari F1 pit-wall team — they chose to believe that it must have been the water.

Chapter V · The Homecoming

Though the road between Austria and Bulgaria may be long, once every year the kingdom welcomes its wandering royals home, where food is plentiful, drinks flow freely, The Kazan is filled, and the three forgotten companions eagerly await their annual appearance.

The Blessing

May your journey together be long and glorious. May your love outlast the Seven Kingdoms, your adventures fill many songs, and may no dragon, White Walker, or Ferrari pit-wall strategist ever stand between you.

And when the road grows dark, remember:

One does not simply walk into Mordor. But apparently, one can walk into marriage without inviting three old friends.

Live happily ever after.
